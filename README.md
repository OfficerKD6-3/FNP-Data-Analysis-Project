# Ferns and Petals (FNP) Sales Analysis – Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the sales performance of **Ferns and Petals (FNP)** using Microsoft Excel.

FNP is a gifting company that delivers products for various occasions such as **Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries**.

The objective of this project is to analyze sales, customer spending, product performance, delivery time, cities, and occasions to identify meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze the FNP sales dataset and build an **interactive Excel dashboard** that provides insights into:

* Overall revenue performance
* Monthly sales trends
* Customer spending behavior
* Product performance
* Delivery time
* Top-performing cities
* Revenue by occasion
* Relationship between order quantity and delivery time

## Business Questions / KPIs

The following questions were analyzed as part of the project:

1. What is the **total revenue** generated?
2. What is the **average order and delivery time**?
3. How does **monthly sales performance** vary throughout 2023?
4. Which products are the **top revenue generators**?
5. What is the **average customer spending**?
6. How do the **top 5 products** perform in terms of sales?
7. Which are the **top 10 cities by number of orders**?
8. Does a higher **order quantity affect delivery time**?
9. How does revenue vary across different **occasions**?
10. Which products are most popular for different **occasions**?

## Process

* Data Cleaning: Checked the dataset for missing values, inconsistencies, and incorrect data formats.
* Data Preparation: Prepared relevant fields such as revenue, order quantity, order date, and delivery time for analysis.
* Data Analysis: Used Excel formulas, Pivot Tables, and calculations to analyze sales, customers, products, cities, and occasions.
* KPI Creation: Calculated important metrics such as total revenue, average order value, total orders, and average delivery time.
* Visualization: Created charts to identify monthly sales trends, top products, top cities, and occasion-wise revenue.
* Dashboard: Combined the KPIs and visualizations into an interactive Excel dashboard for easy business analysis.

## Project Insights

* Generated approximately ₹35.21 lakh in total revenue from 1,000 orders.
* August and February were among the highest-revenue months.
* Magnam Set, Dolores Gift, and Harum Pack were among the top revenue-generating products.
* Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generated the highest revenue among the analyzed occasions.
* Ghaziabad and Bareilly recorded the highest number of orders among the listed cities.
* The analysis showed almost no linear relationship between order quantity and delivery time, with a correlation of approximately 0.0035.
* The average order value was approximately ₹3,521, providing an overview of customer spending per order.
